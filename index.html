About me

Hi, I’m MD. Shamim Osman Chowdhury. I’m a fresh Computer Science and Engineering graduate from United International University (UIU), and Software Quality Assurance is where I do my strongest work. I believe good software isn’t just built, it’s tested, questioned, and refined. That mindset drives how I work. My QA toolkit covers manual testing, requirement analysis, API validation, Playwright automation, and clear defect reporting. I read requirements closely to catch gaps before a single line is tested, validate APIs to make sure the data behind the screen is right, automate repetitive checks with Playwright, and write bug reports that developers can act on right away. Outside QA, I explore web and full-stack development, databases, APIs, artificial intelligence, and machine learning. This background helps me understand software from both sides: how it’s built and how it breaks. I’m now looking for a Junior QA / SQA Engineer role where I can contribute to reliable products, learn from experienced teams, and keep growing.
